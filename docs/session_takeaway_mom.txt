────────────────────────────────────────
3) OPTUNA / BAYESIAN OPTIMISATION (HIGH OVERVIEW)
────────────────────────────────────────

Goal: find good hyperparameters (tree depth, learning rate, …) that maximise a score (we used PR-AUC), without trying every possible combination.

Simple comparison:
- Grid / random search → try many settings blindly (slow / wasteful).
- Bayesian optimisation (Optuna) → learn from past trials and suggest the next setting wisely.

The loop (remember this):
1. Try a set of hyperparameters.
2. Train and score the model.
3. Update what we believe “works”.
4. Suggest the next try (mix of exploit best areas + explore uncertain areas).
5. Repeat for N trials, then retrain with the best settings.

Optuna (default TPE) in one line:
It samples more where past good trials lived, and less where bad trials lived.

In our session:
- Separate Optuna runs for Random Forest and XGBoost.
- Compare on hold-out PR-AUC.
- Show the winner and feature importance in Streamlit → Modelling.
